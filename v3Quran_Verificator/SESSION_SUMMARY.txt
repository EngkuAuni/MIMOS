                         CRITICAL INSIGHT
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Your test revealed a FUNDAMENTAL FLAW in LLM-based OCR for verification:

Problem: Vision-Language Models are trained to be "helpful"
         They auto-complete, correct errors, fill gaps
         This DEFEATS the purpose of verification!

Solution: Dual OCR System
         - QariOCR: High accuracy but may hallucinate
         - Tesseract: Raw extraction, no auto-correction
         - Compare both: Detect when QariOCR auto-corrects
         - Alert user: Make informed decision

Result:  TRUE verification engine that detects errors
         instead of hiding them!
